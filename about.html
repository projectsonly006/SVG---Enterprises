about us

SVG Enterprises is an India-based procurement consultancy and engineering sourcing company helping international and domestic organizations identify, evaluate, source and manage reliable Indian suppliers for industrial products, engineering components and project requirements.

Our Mission

  • Identify capable Indian manufacturers and suppliers
  • Obtain competitive and technically compliant quotations
  • Reduce procurement costs
  • Improve supplier quality and delivery performance
  • Minimize the time and resources required to establish an Indian supply chain
